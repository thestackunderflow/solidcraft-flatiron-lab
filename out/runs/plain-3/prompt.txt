Use the solidcraft MCP tools. /lab/data/flatiron.svg is a drawing of the Flatiron Building's footprint in New York, made from OpenStreetMap data: one unit in the drawing is one metre, and north is up. OpenStreetMap's height tag for the building is 86 (metres). Make a 1:1000 scale model, so 1 metre becomes 1 millimetre: import the drawing into a sketch on the XY plane, extrude it to the right height, and export it as STL to /lab/out/flatiron.stl. Report the footprint area in square millimetres, the bounding box and the height.
